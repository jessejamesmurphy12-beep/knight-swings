Phase here:

Fastest: scrub to the first frame of the swing and tap Auto-track (AI). It finds the head, hips, shoulders and feet on its own, so there are no clicks to line up. It needs this page hosted on your own site (GitHub Pages is fine) and internet the first time. In catcher view, say which foot is the front foot. The click method below is the backup. 1) Load a side-on video and calibrate: click two ends of something with a known width (home plate is 17 in). 2) Scrub to the first frame of the swing and click Head, Belt buckle and Front foot once each. For a tough foot, also scrub to where it lands and click it there – the tracer follows the path in between. 3) Hit Track swing. Load and Foot plant fill in automatically (marked ~); scrub to the true frame and tap a phase button to correct. To fix drift, click the right spot on a bad frame and Track swing again. Catcher view: if the camera is behind the catcher, switch View to Catcher and calibrate across the width of the plate. The table then shows side-to-side (↔) and up/down (↕) movement instead of movement toward the pitcher, since depth can’t be measured from behind.
